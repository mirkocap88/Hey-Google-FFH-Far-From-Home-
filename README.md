# HeyGoogleFFH - Smart Google Assistant & Gemini Home Muter

If you own Google Home / Nest Mini devices at home, you probably know how annoying it is to say "Hey Google" and have your smartphone wake up at the exact same time, interfering with your smart speakers. Plus, if you miss the speed of the old Google Assistant over the slow new Gemini, HeyGoogleFFH is the ultimate utility you need!

---

##English Description

### What is HeyGoogleFFH?
HeyGoogleFFH is an advanced Android utility designed to automatically mute and block Google Assistant / Gemini  on your smartphone when you are inside your home perimeter, while keeping it fully ready when you are away.

### Who is this for?
- **Smart Home Owners**: Anyone with Google Home Mini or Nest speakers who is tired of their phone screen waking up and responding alongside their smart speakers.
- **Android Power Users**: Users who want precise control over when voice assistants are active based on GPS location (Geofencing).

### Key Features:
- **GPS Geofencing**: Automatically detects when you enter or leave up to 2 configured home/work locations.
- **Automatic Muting (Accessibility Service)**: Instantly blocks/dismisses the assistant in the background when you are at home.
- **Manual Master Toggle**: Instantly enable or disable the assistant manually right from the status tab.
- **Privacy-Focused**: No personal data or voice recordings are ever tracked or sent to external servers.

## ⚙️ Setup Instructions / Istruzioni di Configurazione

###How to set up HeyGoogleFFH:
1. **Install the APK**: Install the APK file. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap "More details" and select "Install anyway" in advanced settings during installation.
2. **Permissions Tab**: 
   - First, grant GPS Location permission choosing "Allow all the time".
   - Second, grant Notifications permission.
   - Third, tap on Accessibility, try tapping HeyGoogleFFH (which will currently appear greyed out/untouchable).
   - Fourth, unlock restricted settings via the three dots (⋮) in App Info settings.
   - Finally, go back to Accessibility and tap HeyGoogleFFH to enable it (now clickable).
3. **Location Tab**: Go to the "Posizione Casa" tab, add your home location, and scroll down to save it.
4. **Status Tab**: Return to the Status tab. Everything is done!

###Come configurare HeyGoogleFFH sullo smartphone:
1. **Installa l'APK**: Installa il file APK. Se Google mostra un avviso di sicurezza, vai nelle impostazioni avanzate durante l'installazione e seleziona "Installa comunque".
2. **Scheda Permessi**: 
   - Per prima cosa, dai il permesso alla Posizione GPS scegliendo "Consenti sempre".
   - Dopodiché, dai il permesso per le Notifiche.
   - Entra in Accessibilità e prova a toccare su HeyGoogleFFH (che al momento risulterà grigia e non cliccabile).
   - Sblocca le impostazioni limitate toccando i tre puntini (⋮) in alto a destra nelle informazioni dell'app (App Info).
   - Torna ad Accessibilità e riclicca su HeyGoogleFFH che ora è finalmente cliccabile per attivarla.
3. **Scheda Posizione Casa**: Vai nella scheda "Posizione Casa", aggiungi la posizione di casa e scorri verso il basso per salvare la posizione.
4. **Scheda Stato**: Torna alla scheda di stato ed è tutto fatto!
